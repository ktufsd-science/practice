How to write for credit

  1. Answer the question that is asked. Circle the verb. Describe, explain, evaluate, and identify ask for different things.
  2. Use the data. Copy specific numbers and units from the table or graph. A number with a unit is stronger than the word "increased."
  3. Name the biology idea. Use the science term that explains why, such as feedback mechanism, carrying capacity, natural selection, or amino acid.
  4. Connect them. Use a linking word like because, so, or which means to show how the data support your idea.
  5. Stop. Extra facts do not earn extra credit. Check each part once, then move on.
